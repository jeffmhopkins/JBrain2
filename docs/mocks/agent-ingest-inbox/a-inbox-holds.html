Half of that is already the rule: the tile badge gates on reviewCount > 0 (Launcher.tsx:370-371). The segment pill is not — ReviewScreen.tsx:534 renders it whenever the count is defined, so an empty lane shows a 0 today. Gating it is a small deliberate change, not existing behaviour.

What A commits to. Review stays a place you go, and the count lives only on the launcher tile, so nothing counts at you on home or in the top bar. Two things that behave differently — a thread you reply to and a card you decide — share one screen and one word. In exchange, the inline answer chips make the tab worth opening: the nine-day question is one tap from gone. Build cost is the smallest of the three: relabel the shipped segmented filter, add a threads-waiting endpoint plus a row-level answer control, and leave the block registry alone.

What the mock does not pretend. The wiki cards offer only the two verbs their producers actually file, and the three EMR cards are drawn with the empty proposal list they really have today. The gaps those expose are named in the README, not papered over here.
