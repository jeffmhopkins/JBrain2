And a seam: placement keys on the note’s age, not the question’s. Inference is local-only and a first pass can take minutes, so a note near the two-day edge — and every backdated import, like the 12 Aug records note in frame 2 — has already left the window by the time its question exists, and never appears on home at all. The stream is the fast path; the inbox is the guaranteed one.

What C commits to. The common case stops being a destination at all: you see the question while scrolling past the note that caused it, and one tap ends it.
