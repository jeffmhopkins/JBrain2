The Review tile retires (drawn dashed only to show where it was). With it goes the one place the system could say “there are things outstanding” without being asked — which is either the point, or the problem.

Coupling to flag: B assumes note conversations are listed in the chats picker at all. That is the sibling gate (docs/mocks/agent-ingest/) landing on its option C. If the owner picks A or B there, this variant has no host.

What B commits to. One list of conversations, one mental model: a thread you owe a reply to is a chat, and it sits with the other chats. Genuinely the quietest — no launcher badge, no chrome, nothing on home, and no count on the bucket itself. In exchange, the wiki findings are homed inside Chats where they have no natural business, Review stops being a destination, a Waiting row cannot show its question, and a thread can age indefinitely in a bucket the picker deliberately never lands on.
