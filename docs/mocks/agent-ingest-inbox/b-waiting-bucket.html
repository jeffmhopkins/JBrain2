The Review tile disappears from the launcher and takes its badge with it. Nothing counts anywhere until you open Chats and land on the bucket you chose.

The Review tile is retired (drawn dashed here only to show where it was). With it goes the one place the system could say “there are things outstanding” without you asking — which is either the point, or the problem.

Coupling to flag: B assumes note conversations are listed in the chats picker at all. That is the sibling gate (docs/mocks/agent-ingest/) landing on its option C. If the owner picks A or B there instead, this variant has no host.

What B commits to. One list of conversations, one mental model: a thread you owe a reply to is a chat, and it sits with the other chats. The quietest of the three — no launcher badge, no chrome, nothing on home; the count exists only on a segment you chose to look at. In exchange, the wiki findings are homed inside Chats where they have no natural business, Review stops being a destination, and a question can age indefinitely in a bucket nobody taps.
