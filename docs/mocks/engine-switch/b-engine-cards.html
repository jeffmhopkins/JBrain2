Engine switch · B — two engine cards, tap to make active
The Ops Local engine card holds two half-width engine cards (the settled status-card grid). Each shows what it would cost and deliver; tap the stopped one to make it active — a center confirm dialog states the consequence. During the switch both cards narrate their side (draining → stopped / starting → smoke) over a five-step stepper. Turn on Fail next switch for the rollback. LLM settings groups models by engine. Timings ~12× faster.
