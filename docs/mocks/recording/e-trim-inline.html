E — Trim is the scrub bar. There is no trim mode and no second surface. Tap a row and it opens into a player; the two ends of the scrub bar are handles. Pull one in and you have trimmed — the bar you were already using to find the good bit is the same bar that keeps it.

The claim is that trimming and listening are one activity. You do not know where to cut until you have heard it, and every other shape makes you leave the player, decide, and come back. Here the playhead and the cut live on the same 58 pixels: scrub to the moment it starts, drag the left cap to the playhead, done.

Its risk is the same economy. A handle on a scrub bar is a small target beside a big one, and Apply stays disabled until something actually moves so a fumbled scrub can never silently shorten a recording.
