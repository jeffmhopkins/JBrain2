F — Trim by what was said. Every clip is transcribed on arrival, and the transcript already carries a time for each word. So the transcript can be the trimmer: tap the first word you want to keep, then the last, and the cut lands on those timestamps. The waveform underneath is a readout, not a control.

Nobody thinks about a recording in seconds. They think “the bit where it gives the solar flux”. This is the only shape that lets you say that — and because the transcript feeds hybrid search, the words you trimmed to are the same words you will later search for.

Its honest limit is on screen: the 121.500 clip has no speech, so there is nothing to tap. Any shape that trims by transcript still needs the waveform for the clips that are tones, silence, or a badly-copied signal — which on shortwave is a great many of them.
