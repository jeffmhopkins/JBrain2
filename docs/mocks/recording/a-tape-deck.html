A — The tape deck. Recording is a button on the transport, next to the radio it records. Press it and the box starts writing what you are already hearing; press it again and the clip lands in the library. A recording is a file: a frequency, a time, a length, a size.

This is the shape the plan already assumes, and the one nobody has to be taught. Its weakness is that it only captures what you decided to keep before it happened — on shortwave the interesting thing is usually already over by the time your thumb arrives.
