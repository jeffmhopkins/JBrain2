B — The rolling buffer. The box is always holding the last two minutes of whatever you are listening to. The main control is not “start recording” — it is “save what just happened”.

This is the shape the hobby actually has. A number station starts before you find it; a repeater exchange is over in nine seconds; the interesting thing on shortwave is always already in the past by the time your thumb arrives. A record button asks you to predict; a buffer does not.

It costs about a megabyte. The sidecar already encodes 64 kbps MP3, so two minutes of it is 960 kB of RAM — and the strip below draws the audio level across that window, so you can see the transmission and drag a selection around it.
