What changes underneath, for all three. Sampling moves out of the sheet and into the audio module, so the tape records whenever the radio is playing rather than only while you are looking at it. That costs a small timer instead of an animation frame — drawing still happens only while the sheet is open. One honest limit: the analyser can only be attached after a tap somewhere in the app, so the very first open after a session starts may still come up empty. Every open after that has history.
