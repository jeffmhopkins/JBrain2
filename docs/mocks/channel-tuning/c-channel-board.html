C — The channel board. The scanner idiom: the channels ARE the screen, and the picture shrinks to a ribbon that says only “the band is alive, over there”. Because the board is a list rather than a picture, it can reorder — sort by busiest and the six CB channels anyone is on rise to the top of a forty-channel plan. Tap the × on a tile to skip a channel you never want offered. Switch to FM to see the hatched half the radio is not watching.
