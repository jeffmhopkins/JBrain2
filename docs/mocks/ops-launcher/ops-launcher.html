Ops, reorganised

Live vitals, one Update button and the history charts stay open at the top. Everything else moves behind launcher tiles, the same pattern as the new Settings grid. Tap the tiles, the red banner and Update to try it.

Stays on top

  • GPU, memory, power and disk as current numbers with meters, plus load and uptime.
  • A banner that only appears when a service group is degraded or down, and opens Services.
  • One Update bar: tap, confirm, watch the log, reload.
  • History charts (GPU, power, network, disk I/O) with the range picker.

Try a failure

Moves behind tiles

  • Services: the groups below, per-service restart, start and logs, and Restart all (no longer a header button).
  • Memory: the per-process breakdown.
  • Engine: Flash-Next status and the two prompt-cache settings.
  • Minecraft, Panels, Host (kernel settings), Runs.
  • Storage: database size, notes, files, blobs, swap and fans.

Off is grey

  • A service is off when it is stopped and either opt-in (comfyui, jcode, sdr, mqtt) or the engine you didn't pick (local-llm while Flash-Next runs). It shows a grey dot and a Start button, and never counts against its group.
  • A stock service that stops, or anything that crashes or goes unhealthy, is still red down and raises the banner.

Service groups, re-sorted

  • Core (6): api, worker, supervisor, db, proxy, cloudflared. Proxy and the tunnel join Core because without them you can't reach the box.
  • Models (6): flash-next, embed, tts-stt, rapidocr, local-llm, comfyui. Flash-Next was stranded in Other; rapidocr was in Infra.
  • Assistant tools (8): searxng, reader, byparr, browser, egress, htmlrender, pysandbox, jcode. Most of these sat in Infra or Other.
  • Devices (3 here, 5 with MQTT on): wall, endpoint, sdr, mqtt, mqtt-ingest.
  • Apps (2): minecraft, jlaunch.
  • One-shot jobs: migrate and wipe. They only appear while one is running.
  • "AI - Optional", "Infra", "Display" and the catch-all "Other" go away. Other stays only as a safety net for a service nobody has assigned yet.
