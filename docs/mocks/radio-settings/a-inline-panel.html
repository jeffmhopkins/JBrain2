Bars are signal-to-noise measured on this radio, 162.550 through the fixed listen chain. 10–30 dB is one flat plateau; 0 dB is 20 dB deafer and 40 overloads. With an amp ahead of the tuner the right rung is lower, not higher.

Auto gain invents stations on this radio. Measured on air, three sweeps each way over 162.3–162.7:
  • Auto: seven signals — a phantom at 162.35 and a spur comb at ±55.5 / 111 / 166 / 222 kHz. The real station read 11 dB over the floor.
  • Pinned at 30 dB: one signal, ~29 dB over the floor, no comb.
No gain control down here. Below 24 MHz the radio powers the tuner down and wires the antenna straight to the ADC — every gain stage an R820T2 has is inside the tuner, so there is nothing left to set. This is stored, not applied.
