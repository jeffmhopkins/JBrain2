# GrowBot → a master and the walks it learned from

Kind: Living (mock set) · Status: **picked — B, teachers fold under the master (owner, 2026-11-16)**; binding spec in `docs/reference/DESIGN.md` "GrowBot — the desk robot's screen" · Last verified: 2026-11-16

The owner's ask (2026-11-16): *"It might be even cool to have like a master policy with an
expansion that shows all those sub-policies that went into"*. A master is distilled from three
kept walks (look, forward, back), and its exported file names them in
`training.master.teachers`, so the box can show the family without a new column.

Open `master-family.html` directly in a browser. It is standalone and uses the G2 mock set's
tokens (`../growbot-policies/shared.css`). The dashed bar switches variant and theme; tap a
master to open it.

## The two

- **A — open in place.** Masters get their own section and each opens to show its teachers;
  the single walks list is unchanged, with a note of which masters use each.
- **B — teachers fold under the master.** **Picked.** The sheet is organised by master: a
  walk that taught a master is listed only inside it (under each, when several share it), and
  *Other walks* holds what no master names. Fewer rows; a teacher is one tap deeper but keeps
  all its controls there.
