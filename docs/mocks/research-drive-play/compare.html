Research card — drive-friendly play button

Today's read-aloud control is the 26px icon beside copy/download (see your screenshot). Each variant below keeps the same card — question, chips, Show report, sub-agent roster — and only changes how play is presented. Tap the play controls: they simulate speaking with a fake progress clock. Copy and download stay small; they don't matter in a car.

A full-width 56px amber bar under the question: big disc + "Play brief" + estimated length. While speaking it becomes the progress bar. The whole bar is the tap target. Least change to the card.
