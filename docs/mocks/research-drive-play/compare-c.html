Drive mode — three ways to make "skip story" obvious

In the first version of C, the back/skip buttons were the same grey circles as each other and only differed by a small glyph, so they were hard to tell apart at a glance. Each variant below makes skipping a story look clearly different from pause, and says where you'll land. Tap Play brief in each, then try skip and back. The clock runs fast so progress visibly moves.

C1Labelled story buttons
Pause stays a big round button on its own row. Underneath are two full-width rectangular buttons that name the story they jump to: "Previous · Top national headlines" and "Next · Space: NASA…" (or "Restart" once you're a few seconds into a story). Round means pause, rectangles mean change story, and the words remove the guesswork.

C2Chapter bar
The progress bar is split into one segment per story, so every skip visibly jumps a whole block (you can also tap a segment). Skip buttons are big blue squares as tall as pause, marked "Prev story" and "Next story". They're a different colour and shape from the amber pause.

C3Story list
The player is the list of stories. The current story expands into an amber row with its own pause button. Finished stories turn grey with a ✓, and the ones coming up are rows you can tap to jump to. A single full-width "Next story" button sits at the bottom; there's no back button, but you can tap any earlier row.
