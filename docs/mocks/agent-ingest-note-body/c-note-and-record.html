C · the note stays a note; the writes become a ledger
The Note tab is protected: it opens showing exactly what you captured, and everything added since is folded into one quiet row — 3 clarifications · added in conversation — that expands into a dated log. Clarifications read as a record of additions, not as more of your prose, so you can always tell your writing from your answers without reading carefully. Writes don't appear in the note at all: the Analysis tab becomes Record, where every entity modified chip is collected and grouped by entity rather than scattered through a transcript — and the open question sits at the head of it, carrying the tab's amber count. Answer it in frame 3 and watch the log in frames 1 and 2 take the new block.

Each block says when, what it was answering, and what it changed. The question is quoted with the answer, so a block that reads oddly on its own — “both ankles, worse on the right” — still makes sense a year later. The re-analysis reads the whole stack: body first, then the blocks in order.

What C gives up: the agent's side of it. There is no “here's what I did and why” prose on this screen — you get your answer and a count, and the reasoning lives in the conversation layer behind talk about this note. If you want the agent's account of a decision, you have to go and get it.
