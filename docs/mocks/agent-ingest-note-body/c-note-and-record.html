C protects the note: the Note tab opens on exactly what you captured, and everything added since folds into one quiet row — 1 clarification · added in conversation — that expands into a dated log where each block quotes the question it answered. Clarifications read as a record of additions, never as more of your prose. The tab bar changes here, and only here: Record replaces Analysis and absorbs all of it — the generated title and tags, the salient facts (which are now literally the write ledger, grouped by entity), the entity chips, the wiki backlinks, and the settled Sources card at its foot. Attachments keeps its name and its manifest. Answer the question in frame 3 and watch the log in frames 1 and 2 take the block.

Each block says when, what it was answering, and what it changed. The question is quoted with the answer, so a block that reads oddly on its own — “both ankles, worse on the right” — still makes sense a year later. Re-analysis reads the whole stack: body first, then the blocks in order.

What C gives up: the agent's side of it. There is no “here's what I did and why” prose on this screen — you get your answer and a count. The reasoning is in the conversation, one row away, and you have to go and get it.
