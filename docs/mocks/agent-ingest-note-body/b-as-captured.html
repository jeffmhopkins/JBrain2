Two things to weigh. The good: the note never grows. Whatever the conversation does, this tab opens on three paragraphs, and one control — not a reading habit — tells you whether you are looking at your words or at your words plus your answers. The cost: the strip is a flat chronology. Nineteen rows in time order answers “what happened when” and not “what does this note say about Me” — the entity has to be reassembled in your head, and the same entity appears under four different time rules.
