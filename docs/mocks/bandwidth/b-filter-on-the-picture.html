B — Drag the filter on the picture. No new row. The shaded box on the tuning strip is the control: drag either handle and the filter follows, snapping to the sensible widths (the tick marks along the bottom). The width reads out live while you drag.

The argument for it: the filter and the interference are the same picture, so you are not choosing a number and hoping — you are dragging an edge until the amber station at +5 kHz is outside. The argument against: it needs a steady finger on a phone, and there is no way to see the ladder without touching the radio.
