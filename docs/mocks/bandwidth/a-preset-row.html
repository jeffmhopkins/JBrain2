A — Bandwidth is a row under mode. A second segmented row, the same shape as the MODE row above it, holding the filter widths that make sense for the current mode. One tap, no hidden state, and the ladder re-labels itself when the mode changes.

Watch the picture. The shaded box is what the demodulator hears. The amber signal at +5 kHz is the neighbouring broadcaster. At 8 kHz it sits inside the box — that is the interference you can hear. Tap down to 6 kHz and it falls outside.

Today AM is 16 kHz and is not on this ladder, because it is strictly worse than 8: measured on the real chain, 16 and 8 give identical audio out to 3.5 kHz, and 16 gives the neighbour a free 15 dB. The widest rung becomes the new default.
