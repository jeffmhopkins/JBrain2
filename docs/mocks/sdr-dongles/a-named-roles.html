What will actually happen

Why a description and not just a name: the antenna is the thing that changes. “Long wire, 9:1 unun, north window” is what makes a reading interpretable later, and what tells you which radio to re-point when a band goes quiet. Why dedicated does not fall back: a service quietly moving to another antenna is the exact failure this screen exists to stop — so unplugging a dedicated radio makes its service wait, visibly, rather than move.
