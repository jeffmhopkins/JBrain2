Pick the Home network screen

Three clickable designs for the PWA screen that shows what the box's ad blocker is blocking. Open each on your phone and tap around: devices open, names open a sheet where you can allow or block them, and the dashed strip at the top switches between working, router-forwards, not-set-up and DNS-down states.

A
Devices firstEach device ranked by how much of its traffic gets blocked. Tap the Bedroom TV to see the LG servers it tried to reach and its recent lookups.
B
Who's being toldLeads with how many reports the box stopped this week, grouped by the company receiving them: Samsung, LG, Roku, Google, Meta, Amazon.
C
Live logA running feed of lookups, blocked-only by default, with a chip per device so you can follow one TV as it phones home.

Example data: a made-up week for this house's devices. Kid devices use placeholder names.
