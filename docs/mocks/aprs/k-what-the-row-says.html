H · the coordinates

The position as transmitted, to four decimals. Nothing computed, nothing assumed.

Keeps total honesty — it is exactly what the station sent, and it works with no other information on the box. Costs readability: it is the least human line on the screen, and two stations a mile apart look nearly identical at a glance.

J · the grid square

The Maidenhead locator, six characters, computed from the frame alone — no network, no other data. EL98 is the Space Coast.

Keeps the offline guarantee, and it is how hams already talk about where a station is. Costs precision a non-ham can use: EL98oo means nothing unless you read grids, and it is a 3×4 mile box, not a point.

‹Radio

K · range and bearing

How far away it is and in which direction, from your box — the position the location core already holds and the geofence triggers already read.

Keeps the question a heard log actually answers: how far away is this? Costs a dependency — with no position set for the box the line has to fall back to H or J, so this shape is really “K, then one of the others”.
