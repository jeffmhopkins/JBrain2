What it costs. The question scrolls away with the conversation, so on reopening a long chat it is easy to lose what is being waited on — the pill is the only anchor, and a second question would have nowhere to go. There is also no record in the transcript that a question was ever asked once it is answered. What it buys: no new component, no new view to register, and answering costs exactly one tap in the place your thumb already is.
